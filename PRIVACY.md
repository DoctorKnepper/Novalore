# Novalore privacy policy

_Last updated: October 25, 2026_

Novalore is a personal review journal for books, stories, movies, TV, anime, manga and games. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: **everything stays on your device, and nothing is sent to the developer.**

## What Novalore stores

Everything you enter (titles, creators, ratings, reviews, dates, progress, favorites and the cover images you add or look up) is saved **only on your device**, in the app's private storage. Novalore has no accounts, no servers of its own, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. The developer cannot see your library.

Your appearance settings and sharing preferences are also stored only on your device.

## When Novalore uses the internet

Novalore connects to the internet only when you ask it to:

- **Looking something up** (for a cover, year, creator or length, or to fill in a show's seasons and episodes). The title you search for, and the creator if you entered one, is sent to the service that suits that kind of story:
  - [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org/developers/api) and [Google Books](https://developers.google.com/books) for books and comics
  - [TVmaze](https://www.tvmaze.com/api) for TV shows and anime
  - [AniList](https://anilist.co/) for anime and manga
  - [Wikipedia](https://www.wikipedia.org/) for movies, documentaries and games
- **Fetching cover images** that those services provide.

These requests contain only what's needed for the search. They don't include your reviews, ratings or any account information. Each service handles requests under its own privacy policy, and, like any website, can see your device's IP address.

## Sharing

When you tap **Share**, Novalore hands the review text, and the picture you chose, to the app you pick in Android's share menu. Novalore doesn't send it anywhere else. The review text is also copied to your clipboard, because some apps (such as Instagram and Facebook) ignore text passed to them.

## Backups and exports

- **Your own files:** *Export backup* and *Export CSV* save a file wherever you choose. Importing reads a file you pick. These files never leave your device unless you move them.
- **Android backup:** if Android backup or device transfer is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include Novalore's library and covers in the backup it saves to your Google account, or in a transfer to a new phone. This is handled by Android and Google under your Google account's settings, not by Novalore. You can turn it off in your phone's system settings.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a work or review in the app removes it from your device. Uninstalling Novalore, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it stored. Because the developer never receives your data, there is nothing held elsewhere to delete.

## Children

Novalore is intended for people aged 13 and older. It doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
